Meet Orbi.
Your Claude Code companion.

Orbi is a small white bot that lives in the corner of your Mac's screen. Click it, ask Claude Code something, and watch it work.

Download for Mac Watch the demo

Free · macOS 14+ · Apple silicon (M1 or newer) · No servers, no account

Small bot. Big view of what Claude is doing.

Click, ask, done

Click Orbi or press ⌃⇧Space from any app. Short replies appear right in his speech bubble.

Quick-ask bar

⌃⌘Space opens a round input capsule. Type, press Return, pick the model and folder.

Acts out the work

He scans while reading, taps while editing, peers around while searching and hunkers down while running a command.

Answer permissions in place

When Claude wants to run something, a card offers Allow, Always or Deny, with a chime so you don't miss it.

Watches the Claude app

Follows your conversations, shows each step, and tells you when one finishes. One click jumps to it.

Helpers pop out

When it starts sub-agents, a tiny Orbi appears beside him for each one and hops away when it's done.

Usage at a glance

A thin bar above his name tag shows your session or weekly limit, and warns you at 80% and 95%.

Full chat window

Click the menu-bar icon for saved conversations, formatted replies, code blocks, light and dark looks.

Eats your files

Drag a file or photo onto Orbi and he eats it. It goes along with your next message.

Install in a minute

  1. 1

    Download Orbi-…-macOS-arm64.zip from the latest release and unzip it.

  2. 2

    Move Orbi.app to /Applications.

  3. 3

    First time only: right-click the app, choose Open, then click Open. If macOS still refuses, go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

You need a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later; it does not run on Intel Macs. Orbi isn't signed with an Apple developer account, so macOS asks once. You also need Claude Code installed and signed in (run claude /login once).

Good to know

Does it cost anything?

No. Orbi is free to use and to share (it is not open source). It talks to the Claude Code already installed on your machine, so your own Claude plan applies.

Does Orbi send anything anywhere?

No telemetry and no accounts. The only thing Orbi does on the network is ask GitHub, about once a day, whether a newer version is out (you can switch that off), and download it when you accept an update.

Does Orbi see my login?

No. Usage limits are read by asking Claude Code itself, so Orbi never sees your credentials.

Can it approve things without me?

No. Permission alerts only pass Claude's request to Orbi so you can answer. It can't approve, deny or delay anything on its own.

Why does it ask for Accessibility access?

Only for the optional features that read or type into the Claude app window. Everything else works without it.
